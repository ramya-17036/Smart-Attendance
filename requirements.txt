Mongkoljaturong et al. [1] created an AI-powered MetaHuman interviewer to help students prepare for job interviews. They used a Large Language Model (LLM), speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and speech-to-animation technologies to generate dynamic questions, realistic interactions, and AI-powered feedback. They tested the system with undergraduate students with different career interests and found that it was positively reviewed in terms of usability, interview preparation, and general satisfaction. However, the system had several limitations, including the speech-processing flaws, response delay, dependence on online API, and a small sample size of the evaluation. The authors focused on creating a realistic simulation of an interview rather than a comprehensive career preparation system with features such as resume evaluation, job-role recommendation, communication practice, and performance tracking.
